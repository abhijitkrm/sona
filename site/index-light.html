01 · Rules

Design Principles

The defaults are a pure-black canvas, white-alpha text, and one geometric sans. Hairlines and fills carry structure; a starfield carries the sky. Light mode inverts to eggshell and paper, where navy is the only chromatic voice.

  1. 01

    Void or eggshell

    Page background is pure #000 on screen or #f1f4f4 in light sections. Never a mid-gray canvas.

  2. 02

    Hierarchy by alpha

    Text levels are white at fixed opacities, 100 to 30. A gray hex or a second hue for text is drift.

  3. 03

    No gradient treatments

    The starfield is the only sky. Flat navy carries emphasis; blurred orbs and gradient bands are retired.

  4. 04

    Flat surfaces

    Cards are a fill and a hairline. Hover inverts or brightens; it never lifts. Shadows exist only on light-mode cards.

  5. 05

    Radius discipline

    10 / 20 / 30 / pill, and nothing between. Twenty pixels is the workhorse card radius.

  6. 06

    Print flattens

    Alpha tokens become registered solids on paper. No 8-digit hex ships to WeasyPrint.

02 · Origin

Why it exists

Every generated page drifts the same way: gray text on near-black, a new accent per feature, shadows pretending to be depth. Sona is the correction. One token register, one spec, and templates that compose only what the register holds, so the hundredth page looks like the first.

08 · Drift

What to Avoid

Exceptions are allowed, but the reason should be explicit.

Gray text on dark

Never#999999 or #aaaaaa for body copy. On pure black it is identical to alpha, but it stays put on every surface below.

InsteadUse the alpha ladder: --ink-60 for body, --ink-50 for labels. White alpha adapts to whatever fill sits under it.

Gradient everywhere

NeverNebula on buttons, chips, card rims, or body text. Saturation at small sizes reads as noise.

InsteadNo gradient treatments at all. The starfield carries the sky; flat navy carries emphasis.

Lifted cards

NeverShadow or translateY on a dark card. There is no light source in the void; elevation is a fill step.

Instead--surface-2 over --void with a --stroke-13 rim. Hover steps to --surface-3.

Alpha in print

Never8-digit hex or rgba() in a print template. WeasyPrint draws padding and glyphs at different opacity: double rectangles.

InsteadFlatten to --print-ink-60 (#999999) and siblings, registered in tokens.json.

Off-scale radii

NeverA 14px or 24px card, or a square button. The scale is the whole gesture.

Instead10 / 20 / 30 / 999. If a surface needs more presence, fix the fill or the label, not the radius.

Hover lift

NevertranslateY(-2px) plus shadow on a pill. Motion that imitates a physical button fights the flat system.

InsteadInvert fill and foreground over 300ms, or brighten the alpha fill one step.
