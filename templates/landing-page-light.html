06 · FAQ

Common questions

Is there a free tier?
Yes. Up to 10 seats, full features, no time limit. Paid plans start when you need more seats or controls.
Can we import from our current tools?
One-click importers cover the common project and doc tools, and the open API handles the rest.
Where is our data hosted?
In the region you choose, encrypted at rest and in transit, with full export available anytime.
